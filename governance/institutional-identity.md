# Axodus Institutional Identity

Status: Phase 1 controlled identity record

## Purpose

This document records the current public identity of Axodus and the editorial
decision to retire legacy DAAS/ETF positioning from current institutional
descriptions. It is a terminology control, not a marketing or search-ranking
claim.

## Canonical description

> Axodus is a research-driven initiative developing a proposed ecosystem of
> interoperable, AI-native organizational platforms for governed knowledge,
> institutional workflows, and digital services.

This sentence is a bounded institutional design framing. It does not establish
implementation, integration, production readiness, adoption, users, partners,
revenue, performance, security, regulatory status, or commercial activity.

## Supporting description

Axodus is presented as a conceptual, prototype-stage model for federated
modularity, constitutional constraints, bounded local governance, human
oversight, and interoperable institutional capabilities. Named product or
service nuclei may be listed as controlled institutional context, but their
names do not establish that they exist operationally or are ready for use.

## Superseded terminology

The phrase “the first DAAS (DeFi as a Service) ETF system fully focused on the
investor” is retired from current institutional identity. It may be preserved
only in historical records when needed to explain the evolution of the project.
It must not be used as a current slogan, product definition, or evidence of
financial activity.

DeFi, ETF, investor, token, trading, and related financial terms require
narrow, context-specific review. Their presence in cited literature or
historical records must not be interpreted as a claim that Axodus offers
financial services, investment products, returns, or regulated activity.

## Identity consistency rules

- Use the canonical description verbatim in new institutional documents when
  a short definition is needed.
- Describe Axodus as conceptual and prototype-stage where status matters.
- Distinguish design proposals from implemented capabilities.
- Do not use “first”, “unique”, “guaranteed”, “revolutionary”, or comparable
  superiority language.
- Do not infer that a domain name, module name, or public repository implies
  deployment, adoption, validation, or affiliation.

## Audit record

The 2026-08-26 repository audit searched current Markdown and LaTeX sources
for DAAS, ETF, DeFi, investor, and promotional-risk terms. No DAAS/ETF slogan
was found in the root README or active institutional indexes. DeFi remains in
the research bibliography and related-work material as a bounded scholarly or
historical domain reference. Historical worklog and decision records retain
older terminology for traceability and are not current identity sources.
