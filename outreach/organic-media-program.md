# Axodus Organic Media Program

Status: Controlled planning and drafting track; no external publication
authorized by this document

## Objective

Build a consistent, useful, and traceable public record explaining Axodus,
its concepts, and its proposed platform domains through free channels that
depend primarily on editorial effort. The website remains the canonical source;
external channels distribute excerpts, summaries, and links back to it.

This program does not promise traffic, search ranking, indexing, followers,
reputation change, Knowledge Graph inclusion, or altered model responses.

## Channel roles

| Channel | Role | Publishing rule |
|---|---|---|
| `https://axodus.country/` | Canonical articles and pages | Publish here first |
| `https://axodus.medium.com/` | Article distribution and discovery | Use profile/publication only after review; link to canonical source |

Medium and Substack distribution behavior is controlled by those services and
must be treated as an external observation, not a guaranteed result.

## Editorial pillars

1. **Identity** — what Axodus is, what it is not, and its prototype-stage status.
2. **Architecture** — modularity, interoperability, constitutional constraints, and boundaries.
3. **Governance** — accountability, review, contestation, evidence, and human oversight.
4. **Platforms** — Academy, Marketplace, BBA, ACS, and Governance with qualified status language.
5. **Research and practice** — hypotheses, decisions, limitations, and research agenda.

DeFi, Mining, Lotto, DEX, Trading, tokens, and financial topics remain
conceptual and must not include price, return, yield, strategy, adoption,
performance, or operational claims.

Each long article is drafted on the website first. A Medium or Substack
adaptation should normally be a summary or excerpt, not an uncontrolled exact
duplicate. Threads should link to the canonical article and identify whether
each statement is a fact, proposal, hypothesis, or future work.

## Article requirements

Every draft must include:

- title and publication date;
- author/editor attribution;
- one-sentence topic definition;
- claim classification for material statements;
- canonical URL or intended canonical URL;
- links to the identity, taxonomy, or evidence record where relevant;
- explicit limitations and status language;
- no unsupported adoption, partnership, revenue, performance, security,
  regulatory, or production claims.

Recommended length: 900–1,600 words. Longer research papers remain governed by
the publication workflow rather than this media track.

## Thread requirements

Each thread should contain 5–8 short items and follow this structure:

1. context or question;
2. one bounded concept;
3. one evidence/proposal distinction;
4. one limitation or unresolved question;
5. link to the canonical source.

Do not use defensive repetition of “Axodus is not a scam” as an SEO tactic.
If correcting an error, state the verifiable correction and cite the source.

## Review workflow

```text
Topic → claims classification → draft → evidence review
→ editorial/brand review → website publication → adaptation
→ channel publication approval → observation log
```

The reviewer must check `governance/evidence-boundaries.md`,
`governance/institutional-identity.md`,
`governance/institutional-taxonomy.md`, and
`research/claims-register.md` before approval.

External publication requires explicit human authorization for the selected
channel. No account login, publication, comment, submission, or direct message
is authorized by this file.

## Measurement log

Record monthly:

- canonical articles and threads published;
- destination URLs and publication dates;
- internal links added;
- views and referrals where access is authorized;
- Substack subscriptions where visible;
- search queries and snippets observed;
- crawl or structured-data errors;
- recurring reader questions;
- corrections and withdrawals.

Metrics describe observations and do not establish causality or growth.

## First release batch

The first batch should contain only these three reviewed pieces:

1. `What is Axodus?` — canonical identity article;
2. `The Axodus platform ecosystem` — taxonomy and platform relationships;
3. `Constitutional governance, explained` — governance proposal and human oversight.

Do not begin the regulated-domain article until the first batch is reviewed
and the relevant legal/disclosure boundary has been reconfirmed.

## Gate to external publication

Before any external release, confirm:

- website preview approved by a named human reviewer;
- canonical URL is live and indexable where intended;
- external profile description matches the identity record;
- no draft is marked as released;
- links and images resolve;
- content is free of blocked claims and promotional superlatives;
- exact channel, URL, date, and reviewer are recorded.
