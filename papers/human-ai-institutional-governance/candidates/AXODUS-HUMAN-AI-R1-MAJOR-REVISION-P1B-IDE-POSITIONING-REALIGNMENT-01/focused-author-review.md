## 1. Abstract — papel do IDE

**Antes — inglês**

The *Institutional Decision Episode* (IDE) is a bounded integrative representation that relates institutional authority, mandate validity, delegated-action conformity, evidence, contestability, and disposition within and across episodes. A subsequent internal comparison using the same records for three hypothetical cases supported the coherence of this representation but did not isolate a diagnosis exclusive to the IDE. Linked decisions in federated governance suggest a possible context-specific interpretive benefit. Improved reconstruction, inter-analyst reproducibility, and institutional effectiveness remain unestablished.

**Depois — inglês**

As a supporting analytical device, the *Institutional Decision Episode* (IDE) relates institutional authority, mandate validity, delegated-action conformity, evidence, contestability, and disposition within and across episodes. Its use is bounded by the available evidence; its incremental analytical value, reproducibility, and institutional effectiveness remain open to evaluation.

**Depois — português**

Como instrumento analítico auxiliar, o *Episódio de Decisão Institucional* (IDE) relaciona autoridade institucional, validade do mandato, conformidade da ação delegada, evidência, contestabilidade e disposição dentro de episódios e entre eles. Seu uso é delimitado pela evidência disponível; seu valor analítico incremental, sua reprodutibilidade e sua efetividade institucional permanecem abertos à avaliação.

**Efeito:** Subordina o instrumento ao framework no resumo; os resultados detalhados permanecem no texto e na discussão.

**Ambiguidade / decisão autoral:** Nenhuma identificada; sujeita à avaliação do autor.

## 2. Introduction — contribuição e instrumento

It distinguishes mandate validity from action conformity, and capability and configured enablement from institutional authority. Identifiable preparatory acts can be examined where records support them; the representation does not by itself recover influences that left no trace.

## 9. Discussão — realocação sem alteração textual

After the original conceptual synthesis and illustrative reconstructions described in Section 2.5, a separate internal assessment compared the IDE with HAIG, reviewability, and contestable AI by design using a common dossier for the three hypothetical cases (Engin, 2026; Cobbe et al., 2021; Alfrink et al., 2023). The protocol and dossier were fixed before the applications, but the exercise was documentary and interpretive: one informed analyst applied the frameworks without blinding or independent coding, and the dossier included the manuscript’s own IDE classifications and interpretations. The comparison is not peer review, independent validation, or a test of empirical performance.

Diagnostic convergence predominated in procurement (Section 6.3): the comparators could distinguish the defects illustrated by the three variants using the same record. In high-cadence delegation (Section 6.4), contextual applications could also distinguish the standing mandate, authorization, operational action, and external effects. In the federated case (Appendix A.4), linking distinct episodes made the change in competence, the objects of challenge, and their dispositions explicit. Reviewability and contestable AI by design could reach equivalent distinctions on that same dossier. The comparison therefore supports the coherence of the IDE’s representation; it did not isolate an exclusive diagnosis. Potential interpretive value is most apparent where an analyst might otherwise conflate acts or dispositions at an institutional boundary.
