# EPIC E4 — Evidence, traceability, and AI assistance

## Implementation request

Explain evidence, provenance, accountability, and bounded AI assistance as
architectural principles under research. Do not turn cryptographic examples or
human-oversight diagrams into claims that Axodus already implements them.

## Inputs

- `governance/evidence-boundaries.md`
- `research/claims-register.md`
- `governance/publication-review-checklist.md`
- `plans/institutional/organic-media-aees.md`

## Scope and non-goals

In scope: Articles 7 and 8, four threads, conceptual diagrams, limitations,
and research links. Out of scope: immutable evidence, cryptographic security,
legal accountability, autonomous production agents, confidence scores,
kill-switches, rollback, or live anomaly response as existing capabilities.

## Sprint E4-S1 — Evidence and traceability

### E4-S1-T1 — Draft `Evidence and traceability by design`

- Action: present evidence, provenance, and accountability as proposed design
  requirements and explain what a future implementation would need to record.
- Validate: no guarantee of integrity, immutability, security, or legal result.

### E4-S1-T2 — Review cryptographic details

- Action: move signatures, hashes, ledgers, Merkle structures, and ZKPs to
  clearly labelled examples or future research unless a registered source
  supports them.
- Validate: no cryptographic implementation is implied.

### E4-S1-T3 — Produce two limitation threads

- Action: cover privacy/transparency and performance/opacity trade-offs.
- Validate: remove numeric benchmarks and claims of complete reconstruction.

## Sprint E4-S2 — Bounded AI assistance

### E4-S2-T1 — Draft `AI assistance under human oversight`

- Action: define bounded scope, human-in-the-loop approval, human-on-the-loop
  supervision, and responsibility as a proposed model.
- Validate: no autonomous operational capability is asserted.

### E4-S2-T2 — Review runtime-control language

- Action: label confidence scores, policy interceptors, kill switches,
  rollback, and live anomaly response as proposals or future work.
- Validate: no current control plane or deployment is claimed.

### E4-S2-T3 — Produce human-authority threads

- Action: write one approval flow and one supervision flow as illustrative
  scenarios with explicit non-operational status.

## Acceptance criteria

- Articles 7–8 contain evidence classifications, limitations, and canonical
  links.
- Four threads do not claim cryptographic, legal, security, or runtime
  guarantees.
- All AI authority boundaries are explicit and human review remains required.

## Blockers and escalation

Escalate requests to state that a control is deployed, secure, compliant,
audited, or legally accountable without evidence and approval.
