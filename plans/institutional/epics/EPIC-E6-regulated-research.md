# EPIC E6 — Regulated domains and research agenda

## Implementation request

Cover DeFi, Mining, and Lotto as bounded conceptual domains and close the
editorial series with a research agenda and open questions. Apply heightened
review to financial, regulatory, security, and quantitative language.

- `governance/evidence-boundaries.md`

## Scope and non-goals

In scope: Articles 11 and 12, four threads, limitations, and future research
questions. Out of scope: financial advice, strategies, returns, token or price
claims, operational mining/lotto/DeFi services, security guarantees, and
benchmark results.

## Sprint E6-S1 — DeFi, Mining, and Lotto

### E6-S1-T1 — Draft bounded-domain article

- Action: describe the three domains as conceptual test cases for state,
  validation, and randomness questions.
- Validate: explicitly separate research abstraction from a product or service.

### E6-S1-T2 — Review absolutes and regulated language

- Action: remove “absolute”, “tamper-proof”, “cryptographically secure”, and
  similar guarantees unless supported; add legal/disclosure limitations.
- Validate: no financial or security promise remains.

### E6-S1-T3 — Produce two boundary threads

- Action: illustrate technical questions without describing an actual
  transaction, pool, lottery, mining operation, or user outcome.

- Action: write one thread from hypothesis to future benchmark and one from

## Acceptance criteria

- Articles 11–12 use bounded, non-promotional, non-financial language.
- Four threads contain no operational scenario represented as fact.
- Quantitative claims are removed, sourced, or explicitly registered as
  hypotheses awaiting validation.

## Blockers and escalation

Escalate any request involving financial promotion, regulated advice,
security guarantees, quantitative results, or claims of live operation.
