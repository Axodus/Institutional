# EPIC E3 — Governance and proposed nuclei

## Implementation request

Develop the governance series as a set of conceptual proposals: constitutional
governance, proposed platform nuclei, and Governance as a standalone context
with a clearly bounded Harmony reference. Preserve the distinction between
proposal and adoption in every artifact.

## Inputs

- `governance/institutional-taxonomy.md`
- `governance/evidence-boundaries.md`
- `research/claims-register.md`
- `governance/publication-review-checklist.md`

## Scope and non-goals

In scope: Articles 4–6, six threads, conceptual diagrams, and links to the
canonical identity/taxonomy. Out of scope: hard-coded rules, live enforcement,
certification, monetization, production deployment, Harmony adoption,
partnership, or binding decisions.

## Acceptance criteria

- Articles 4–6 clearly use proposal, hypothesis, or future-work labels.
- Six threads and diagrams contain no operational maturity implication.
- Harmony scope is described without asserting external adoption or binding
  governance.

## Blockers and escalation

Escalate legal, financial, certification, or production claims; do not convert
an annotated scenario into evidence of implementation.
