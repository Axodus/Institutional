# EPIC E7 — Distribution, review, and measurement

## Implementation request

Operationalize reviewed adaptations, channel references, monthly observations,
and periodic editorial audits after the canonical series is ready. This EPIC
opens a controlled organic-media production front; it does not authorize
publication, outreach, or external contact by itself.

## Inputs

- `plans/institutional/organic-media-plan.md`
- `plans/institutional/organic-media-aees.md`
- `outreach/organic-media-program.md`
- `governance/publication-review-checklist.md`

## Scope and non-goals

In scope: adaptation queues, GitHub references, observation logging, correction
records, and quarterly audits. Out of scope: paid campaigns, backlinks,
press outreach, community spam, review solicitation, or unapproved external
publication.

## Sprint E7-S1 — Adaptation and release preparation

### E7-S1-T1 — Prepare Medium adaptations

- Action: adapt approved canonical articles as summaries or clearly attributed
  versions with links to the site.
- Validate: no divergence in identity, status, claims, or limitations.

### E7-S1-T2 — Prepare Substack adaptations

- Action: prepare newsletter editions with subject, preview, canonical link,
  date, and an explicit decision about web/e-mail/app distribution.
- Validate: distribution remains disabled until human approval.

### E7-S1-T3 — Prepare GitHub discussion/changelog references

- Action: add documentary references to README, Discussions, Releases, or
  issues only where the repository enables them and the content is approved.
- Validate: GitHub text does not imply implementation, readiness, or adoption.

## Sprint E7-S2 — Observation and correction

### E7-S2-T1 — Record authorized metrics

- Action: log published articles, canonical pages, internal links, channel
  views, subscriptions, references, and editorial corrections where available.
- Validate: observations are dated and do not claim causal growth.

### E7-S2-T2 — Record search observations

- Action: record queries, snippets, dates, correction prompts, and source URLs
  as observations only.
- Validate: no promise of reindexing, ranking, Knowledge Graph inclusion, or
  disappearance of an unrelated result.

### E7-S2-T3 — Run quarterly content audit

- Action: check identity consistency, links, metadata, claims, terminology,
  and outdated status labels across all channels.
- Validate: findings, corrections, and unresolved blockers are logged.

## Acceptance criteria

- Adaptation queues and observation log are ready for each canonical article.
- Channel references are consistent and human approval is recorded before
  release.
- Monthly observations and quarterly audit records distinguish facts from
  interpretations.
- No paid or directed outreach is performed.

## Blockers and escalation

Escalate missing approval, channel access, unavailable metrics, inconsistent
external copy, or a request to contact communities or media.
