# EPIC E1 — Identity and disambiguation

## Implementation request

Produce the first canonical editorial package explaining what Axodus is, its
current conceptual/prototype-stage status, and how it is distinct from other
entities with similar names. Use neutral, factual language and avoid turning
negative search labels into repeated SEO terms.

## Inputs

- `what-is-axodus.md`
- `governance/institutional-identity.md`
- `governance/institutional-taxonomy.md`
- `plans/institutional/organic-media-aees.md`

## Scope and non-goals

In scope: canonical website article, boundary block, one Medium adaptation,
one Substack adaptation, and two short threads. Out of scope: claims of a
commercial enterprise platform, operational benefits, adoption, investment,
or a promise to change search results.

## Sprint E1-S1 — Canonical identity package

### E1-S1-T1 — Draft `What is Axodus?`

- Action: write the canonical article in plain English with identity, scope,
  taxonomy, stage, official links, limitations, and date.
- Output: website draft with stable target path and metadata.
- Validate: matches the identity record; every material statement is classified.

### E1-S1-T2 — Draft “What Axodus is not”

- Action: describe exclusions such as financial-product positioning,
  operational maturity, and unsupported product claims without defensive
  keyword repetition.
- Output: boundary section linked from the canonical article.
- Validate: no use of “scam”, “Ponzi”, or similar terms as a search tactic.

### E1-S1-T3 — Produce Medium adaptation

- Action: create an editorial summary that links to the canonical article and
  preserves conceptual/prototype qualifications.
- Output: reviewed draft; publication remains gated.

### E1-S1-T4 — Produce Substack adaptation

- Action: create a newsletter version with a clear subject, preview, date,
  canonical link, and status note.
- Output: reviewed draft; email/app distribution is not enabled by this task.

## Sprint E1-S2 — Brand disambiguation

### E1-S2-T1 — Write identity thread

- Action: prepare five to eight points covering identity, scope, stage,
  taxonomy, and official sources.
- Validate: no superiority, uniqueness, or outcome claim.

### E1-S2-T2 — Write neutral Axodus/Exodus distinction thread

- Action: distinguish names and domains using neutral references to the
  canonical page; do not repeat harmful labels.
- Validate: thread does not target or amplify negative terms.

### E1-S2-T3 — Add canonical channel links

- Action: align links to `axodus.country`, `docs.axodus.country`, GitHub,
  Medium, Substack, and Notion where ownership is verified.
- Validate: URL and ownership review passes.

## Acceptance criteria

- Canonical article and boundary section are internally reviewed.
- Two threads and both external adaptations link back to the canonical page.
- Unsupported B2B, open-source, efficiency, loyalty, competitiveness, and
  implementation claims are removed or explicitly marked as proposals.
- No external item is published without human approval.

## Blockers and escalation

Escalate contradictions between historical copy and the canonical identity,
uncertain ownership of a channel, or requests for promotional positioning.
