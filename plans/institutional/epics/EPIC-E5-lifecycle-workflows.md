# EPIC E5 — Lifecycle and platform workflows

## Implementation request

Document the proposed Research → Architecture → Documentation → Prototype
lifecycle and the conceptual relationship among Academy, BBA, and
institutional workflows. Keep future review and independent deployment as
open questions, not completed capabilities.

## Inputs

- `governance/institutional-taxonomy.md`
- `governance/institutional-identity.md`
- `governance/evidence-boundaries.md`
- `research/claims-register.md`

## Scope and non-goals

In scope: Articles 9 and 10, four threads, lifecycle diagrams, and internal
navigation. Out of scope: certification, external validation, production
deployment, third-party adoption, independent operation, or claims that BBA
workflows are implemented.

## Sprint E5-S1 — Research to reference platform

### E5-S1-T1 — Draft lifecycle article

- Action: describe the four-stage lifecycle as a planning model from research
  through conceptual prototyping and refinement.
- Validate: each stage is classified; no stage is presented as completed.

### E5-S1-T2 — Bound the reference-platform concept

- Action: explain external review and independent deployment as future work and
  possible evaluation criteria.
- Validate: remove “validates”, “certifies”, and third-party adoption claims.

### E5-S1-T3 — Produce two lifecycle threads

- Action: illustrate idea-to-prototype and documentation-to-independent-test
  flows without asserting execution or readiness.

## Sprint E5-S2 — Academy, BBA, and institutional workflows

### E5-S2-T1 — Draft platform-workflows article

- Action: describe Academy, BBA, and institutional workflows as bounded
  conceptual functions in the taxonomy.
- Validate: no deploy, compliance-code, certification, or production claim.

### E5-S2-T2 — Resolve BBA terminology

- Action: select and record one approved expansion/description for BBA; if the
  acronym remains ambiguous, use “BBA (term under definition)”.
- Validate: no invented product or organizational meaning is treated as fact.

### E5-S2-T3 — Produce relationship threads

- Action: describe qualification, component design, and institutional review
  as proposed relationships rather than operational workflows.

## Acceptance criteria

- Articles 9–10 distinguish research models from implemented systems.
- Four threads and diagrams use future/proposal labels where required.
- BBA terminology is documented and does not imply an unverified product.

## Blockers and escalation

Escalate requests for certification language, external-review status,
independent deployment claims, or unresolved BBA ownership/meaning.
