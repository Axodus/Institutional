# Organic-media editorial inventory

Status: E0 control baseline — planning only; no item is approved or published

This inventory is the production index for the organic-media program. The
website is canonical. An adaptation or thread is not ready until its linked
canonical article has passed human review and has an approved canonical URL.

## Control fields

- **Owner:** `Institutional editorial owner (TBD)` until a named human is
  recorded.
- **Status vocabulary:** `Planned`, `Drafting`, `Evidence review`, `Editorial
  review`, `Canonical approved`, `Adaptation review`, `Authorized`,
  `Published`, `Blocked`.
- **External authorization:** must identify the human, channel, batch, exact
  version, and date. `Pending` is the baseline and does not authorize action.
- **Canonical paths:** intended website paths are planning identifiers until
  the website owner confirms the live URL.
