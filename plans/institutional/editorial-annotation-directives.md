# Editorial annotation directives — Articles 1–12

Status: normative drafting control; annotations are source material, not
approved copy.

## Purpose

The supplied annotations preserve intended concepts and scenarios, but some
wording presents proposals, hypotheses, or future work as established
capabilities. Every article must apply:

annotation → claim classification → evidence check → bounded rewrite →
limitation → human review → approval or block

Use four statuses: Fact; Design proposal; Hypothesis; Future work. Never
promote an annotation directly into public copy. The claims register and
evidence boundaries control final wording.

## Article-specific tone controls

### ART-01 — What is Axodus?

Use the canonical identity and conceptual/prototype-stage status. Describe
governed knowledge, institutional workflows, digital services, modularity, and
human oversight as scope or design topics. Do not describe Axodus as a generic
B2B product, implemented enterprise suite, or source of efficiency, loyalty,
competitiveness, or operational results. DAAS/ETF wording is historical only.

### ART-02 — Why governed knowledge matters

Frame document fragmentation as a bounded research problem. Remove the
unreferenced “20% of time” statistic and claims of precision, real-time
connection, or guaranteed improvement.

### ART-03 — The Axodus platform ecosystem

Present foundation, platforms, nuclei, actors, capabilities, and artifacts as
proposed taxonomy. Cloud, zero trust, event mesh, financial cores, immutable
ledgers, and similar mechanisms are examples or future work unless evidenced.

### ART-04 — Constitutional governance, explained

Explain constitutional governance as a proposed model for principles,
boundaries, review, escalation, and human authority. Do not present automatic
blocking, failsafes, compliance, or runtime enforcement as existing.

### ART-05 — What is a proposed platform nucleus?

Treat ACS, Academy, and Marketplace as proposed nuclei. Do not assert
consolidation, certification, monetization, pricing, liquidation, revenue,
performance, or operational graduation.

### ART-06 — Governance as a standalone platform context

Describe Governance as a proposed standalone context and Harmony as a proposed
Axodus-specific framing. Separate Proposal from possible Adoption. Do not imply
acceptance, autonomous enforcement, binding decisions, production, or
partnership.

### ART-07 — Evidence and traceability by design

Describe evidence, provenance, and accountability as design requirements or
research questions. Signatures, hashes, Merkle trees, ledgers, and ZKPs are
examples or future work unless evidenced. Do not promise integrity, security,
legal accountability, or complete reconstruction.

### ART-08 — AI assistance under human oversight

Describe bounded AI assistance and human oversight as a proposed model.
Confidence scores, interceptors, kill switches, rollback, anomaly response,
and autonomous agents must not be presented as existing controls.

### ART-09 — From research to reference platform

Use Research → Architecture → Documentation → Prototype as a planning model.
External review and independent deployment are future goals, not completed
validation, certification, production readiness, or adoption.

### ART-10 — Academy, BBA, and institutional workflows

Describe Academy, BBA, and institutional workflows as conceptual functions.
Resolve or retain BBA ambiguity explicitly. Do not assert certification,
compliance-as-code, deployment, production workflows, or recertification.

### ART-11 — DeFi, Mining, and Lotto as bounded domains

Keep all three domains technical and conceptual. Remove financial strategy,
returns, operational services, safety or security guarantees, VRF/ZKP
implementation claims, and actual transaction or benchmark scenarios unless
reviewed and evidenced.

### ART-12 — Axodus research agenda and open questions

Use synthesis, limitations, hypotheses, and open questions. The annotated 80%
and sub-50ms values are untested hypotheses or must be removed; they are never
results. Do not imply validated benchmarks, adoption, enforcement, or completed
research.

## Required review record

For every article, record the directive used, changed or removed claims,
evidence decision, limitations, reviewer, and exact approved version. If
evidence is missing, use [REFERENCE NEEDED] internally and block the claim from
public copy.
