# Organic media — image request prompts

Status: controlled creative brief; images require human review before
publication.

These prompts are for editorial illustrations accompanying the twelve
canonical articles in the organic-media program. They are not evidence of
implementation. Use a restrained research-editorial style: clear diagrams,
neutral lighting, generous whitespace, accessible contrast, and no invented
logos, interfaces, user counts, financial outcomes, or operational claims.

## Global image constraints

- Prefer abstract systems diagrams, information architecture, and editorial
  illustrations over product screenshots.
- Do not depict trading screens, coins, price charts, yield, lottery tickets,
  mining rigs, investor dashboards, or implied financial returns.
- Do not add badges such as “verified”, “certified”, “secure”, “live”, or
  “production”.
- Do not invent partner logos, organizations, users, metrics, geographic reach,
  or deployment environments.
- Use “conceptual model”, “proposed”, or “research” labels only when text is
  needed; avoid dense copy inside generated images.
- Export a 16:9 hero crop and a 1:1 social crop. Preserve a text-safe area for
  the title overlay.
- Provide a concise alt text that describes the image without making claims
  about the Axodus system.

## Article 1 — What is Axodus?

**Image request prompt (English):**

> Editorial illustration for a research-driven initiative called Axodus: a
> calm central axis connecting five abstract tiles labeled identity,
> architecture, governance, knowledge, and digital services. Show the tiles
> as proposed conceptual components around a neutral central node, with thin
> lines and ample whitespace. Minimal academic technology aesthetic, deep navy,
> slate, muted cyan, soft off-white background, no crypto imagery, no product
> UI, no people, no claims of deployment or scale, no logos. Leave the upper
> left quadrant clear for a headline.

Alt text: “Conceptual diagram of an axis connecting identity, architecture,
governance, knowledge, and digital services.”

## Adaptation for threads and social posts

For each article, derive the 1:1 crop from the approved canonical image. Do not
create a new visual claim in a thread. If a thread needs a diagram, use the
same labels and status qualifiers as the article, and link to the canonical
page. Record the asset path, alt text, license/source, approval date, and
publication URLs in the editorial observation log.
