# ART-01 threads

## Thread A — Identity

1. **What is Axodus?** Axodus is a research-driven initiative developing a proposed ecosystem of interoperable, AI-native organizational platforms for governed knowledge, institutional workflows, and digital services.

2. **Problem space:** Axodus explores how knowledge, decisions, workflows, responsibilities, and digital services can remain legible in relation to one another.

3. **Proposed scope:** Its ecosystem is described through governance, modularity, human oversight, institutional records, and bounded AI assistance as design themes.

4. **Taxonomy:** The institutional taxonomy maps identity, foundation concepts, proposed platform and service nuclei, role labels, lifecycle ideas, and records.

5. **Current stage:** Axodus is conceptual and prototype-stage. The materials describe proposed relationships and research questions, not an operating platform.

6. **What is not claimed:** The current identity does not claim implementation, adoption, users, partners, revenue, performance, security, certification, or production readiness.

7. **Canonical links:** Read the article at [What is Axodus?](../canonical/article.md), with broader context at [axodus.country](https://axodus.country), [docs.axodus.country](https://docs.axodus.country), and the [Institutional repository](https://github.com/Axodus/Institutional).
